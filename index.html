(01) — About me Curious mind · Mumbai

Curious by nature. Creative by instinct.

Hi, I'm Anushree — a Mumbai designer who is passionate about visual communication: ideas you can see, hold and remember.

Sometimes that's a brand and the box it comes in, sometimes a photograph, sometimes a poster you can't walk past. I collect references like souvenirs, ask too many questions and sketch until the idea clicks — and packaging is where it all comes together: a story you can hold in your hands.
